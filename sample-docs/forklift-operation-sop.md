# Forklift Operation Standard Operating Procedure

This SOP covers the safe operation of counterbalance and reach forklifts at all Wiseway Logistics distribution centres. It applies to every operator, supervisor, and pedestrian working in or near forklift operating areas.

## Licensing and authorisation

- You must hold a current **High Risk Work Licence (HRWL) class LF** (or LO for order-picking trucks) issued by the relevant state regulator (SafeWork NSW, WorkSafe VIC, etc.).
- You must also hold a **site authorisation** from your warehouse supervisor confirming you have been inducted on the specific machine type before operating it.
- Carry your licence while operating. Operating without a current HRWL is grounds for immediate stand-down.

## Pre-start inspection

Before the first use each shift, complete the pre-start checklist on the machine and log it in WiseWork. Check:

1. **Tyres** — condition, inflation, no exposed cords.
2. **Forks and mast** — no cracks, hydraulic leaks, or bent tines; chains tensioned evenly.
3. **Fluids** — engine oil, coolant, hydraulic fluid (or battery charge level for electric units).
4. **Controls and brakes** — service brake, park brake, horn, lights, and beacon all functional.
5. **Seatbelt and overhead guard** — present and undamaged.

Tag out and report any defect immediately. **Do not operate a forklift with a recorded defect** until it is cleared by a supervisor.

## Operating rules

- Always wear your seatbelt. Travel with the load **tilted back and lowered** to approximately 150 mm above the floor.
- Observe the site speed limit (**10 km/h** in the warehouse, **5 km/h** at intersections and dock approaches).
- Sound the horn at blind corners, doorways, and pedestrian crossings.
- Never exceed the rated load capacity on the data plate. Never lift or carry passengers.
- When travelling without clear forward vision because of the load, **travel in reverse** (except when ascending ramps).

## Pedestrian separation

Forklifts and pedestrians must be kept apart wherever practicable. Operators must give way to pedestrians at all times. Pedestrians must use the **marked walkways** and make eye contact with the operator before crossing an aisle. Never walk under raised forks or reach into the path of a moving machine.

## Refuelling and battery charging

- **LPG units:** refuel only in the designated outdoor cage with the engine off and no ignition sources. Check hoses and the cylinder collar before reconnecting.
- **Electric units:** charge in the ventilated battery bay. Wear face shield and gloves when handling batteries. Ensure the charger is off before connecting or disconnecting. Report any acid spill immediately and use the spill kit.

## End of shift

Park on level ground, lower the forks fully, apply the park brake, switch off, and remove the key. Place LPG units on the gas-isolated bay and electric units on charge.

For questions, contact your warehouse supervisor or the WHS team.
