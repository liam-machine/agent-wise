# Annual Leave Policy

This policy explains how annual leave works for permanent Wiseway Logistics employees in Australia. It applies to full-time and part-time staff covered by the National Employment Standards (NES). Casual employees do not accrue annual leave.

## Your entitlement

Full-time employees accrue **four (4) weeks of paid annual leave** for each year of continuous service. Shift workers who meet the definition in the relevant award or agreement accrue **five (5) weeks** per year. Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis according to their ordinary hours.

## How leave accrues

Annual leave accrues progressively throughout the year based on your ordinary hours of work and accumulates from year to year. It begins accruing from your first day of employment, including during your probation period, though you generally cannot take leave until it has accrued. There is no cap on accrual, but Wiseway may direct you to take leave where your balance becomes **excessive** — defined as more than eight (8) weeks accrued (or ten weeks for shift workers) — in line with your award.

## Applying for leave

1. Submit your request through the WiseWork rostering portal at least **two (2) weeks** in advance. Requests covering peak periods (December, June end-of-financial-year, and public holiday long weekends) should be lodged **four (4) weeks** ahead.
2. Your warehouse supervisor or transport manager reviews requests against operational coverage. Approval is not automatic during peak freight periods.
3. You will receive written confirmation in the portal once approved. Do not book travel before your leave is approved.

Leave is taken in agreement with your manager. Wiseway will not unreasonably refuse a request, but coverage of warehouse and driver shifts takes priority during high-volume weeks.

## Leave loading

Employees covered by the **Road Transport and Distribution Award** receive **17.5% annual leave loading** on top of their base rate when taking annual leave, unless the loading is already absorbed into an all-up rate specified in your contract. Loading is calculated on the ordinary rate for the leave taken and appears as a separate line on your payslip.

## Cashing out and payment on termination

You may request to cash out annual leave only where you retain at least four weeks accrued after the cash-out, with a separate written agreement for each occasion. On termination, any untaken accrued annual leave (including applicable loading) is paid out in your final pay.

## Public holidays during leave

If a public holiday falls within a period of approved annual leave, that day is treated as a paid public holiday and is **not** deducted from your annual leave balance.

For questions, contact the HR team or your line manager.
