# Vehicle Pre-Start Checklist SOP

Every Wiseway Logistics driver must complete a daily pre-start (pre-trip) inspection before operating a rigid or articulated vehicle. This is a Chain of Responsibility (CoR) obligation under the Heavy Vehicle National Law (HVNL) and a condition of driving for Wiseway. Record the completed check in WiseWork before you leave the depot.

## When to do it

- Before the **first trip** of each shift, and
- After any change of vehicle or trailer during the shift, and
- Any time you have reason to believe the vehicle's condition has changed.

## Daily pre-start checklist

**Outside the vehicle (walk-around):**

1. **Tyres** — tread depth, inflation, no cuts or bulges; wheel nuts and indicators intact.
2. **Lights and indicators** — headlights, brake lights, indicators, hazards, and beacon all working.
3. **Body and load** — no fluid leaks under the vehicle; mirrors clean and adjusted; load restraints (straps, gates, twist-locks) secure and rated.
4. **Coupling** — fifth wheel locked, safety clip engaged, airlines and electrical leads connected and undamaged.
5. **Number plates and signage** — clean and legible.

**In the cab:**

6. **Brakes** — service brake and park brake; build air pressure and check for warning buzzer/light clearing.
7. **Gauges and warning lights** — oil, coolant, air, AdBlue, and dashboard fault lights.
8. **Steering, horn, wipers, and washers** — functional.
9. **Seatbelt** — present and not frayed.
10. **Documents** — licence, work diary, and consignment paperwork on board.

## Defect reporting

If you find a defect, classify it:

- **Major defect** (brakes, steering, tyres, coupling, lights at night): **do not drive.** Tag the vehicle out and report to your transport manager immediately for repair or a replacement vehicle.
- **Minor defect** (cosmetic, non-safety-critical): log it in WiseWork and notify the workshop; the manager decides whether the vehicle can run.

Never operate a heavy vehicle with an unrepaired major defect. Doing so breaches the HVNL and the Chain of Responsibility.

## Fatigue management

You are responsible for starting each shift **fit for duty**. Do not drive if you are fatigued, unwell, or affected by alcohol or drugs. Follow your work and rest hours under the HVNL and record them in your work diary. Take your scheduled rest breaks, and if you become too fatigued to drive safely, stop in a safe location and contact your transport manager. Wiseway will not penalise a driver for stopping due to genuine fatigue.

For questions, contact your transport manager or the WHS team.
