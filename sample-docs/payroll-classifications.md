# Payroll Classifications and Pay Rules

**Restricted document — HR/Payroll use only.** This document contains commercially sensitive pay information and must only be accessed by authorised HR and payroll staff. Do not forward or share rates with employees outside the payroll process.

This document summarises how Wiseway Logistics classifies employees for pay purposes and the overtime, penalty, and timesheet rules applied each fortnight. Rates are based on the **Road Transport and Distribution Award 2020** and the **Storage Services and Wholesale Award 2020**, plus any over-award arrangements in individual contracts.

## Classification grades

| Grade | Typical role | Base hourly (full-time, indicative) |
|---|---|---|
| Grade 1 | Entry warehouse / pick-pack | $25.40 |
| Grade 2 | Forklift operator (LF licence) | $27.10 |
| Grade 3 | Senior storeperson / dispatch lead | $28.85 |
| Driver Grade 2 | Rigid (4.5–13.9 t) | $28.20 |
| Driver Grade 4 | Articulated / B-double | $30.95 |
| Office Grade 3 | Customer service / admin | $29.40 |

Grades are reviewed against award minimums each year on the first full pay period after **1 July**. Indicative rates above are pre-tax and exclude loadings, allowances, and superannuation (currently **11.5%**).

## Overtime

- Overtime applies after **38 ordinary hours** per week or **7.6 hours** per day for day workers.
- Rate: **time-and-a-half (150%)** for the first two overtime hours, **double time (200%)** thereafter.
- A minimum overtime engagement and meal allowance apply per the relevant award where overtime exceeds 1.5 hours without notice.

## Penalty rates

| When worked | Loading |
|---|---|
| Saturday | 150% |
| Sunday | 200% |
| Public holiday | 250% |
| Afternoon shift (finish after 6pm) | +15% shift loading |
| Night shift (commence after 11pm) | +30% shift loading |

Penalty rates do not compound with overtime; the higher single rate applies.

## Allowances

Common allowances include the **cold-chamber allowance** (freezer work), **leading-hand allowance**, **first-aid allowance**, and **meal allowance** for unplanned overtime. Drivers may receive a **travel/away-from-base allowance** for overnight linehaul. Allowances are listed separately on the payslip.

## Timesheet cutoffs

- The pay cycle is **fortnightly**, Monday to Sunday, paid the **Thursday** after cycle close.
- Timesheets must be approved by the manager in WiseWork by **10:00 am Monday** following the close of the fortnight.
- Late timesheets are processed in the **next** pay run; manual off-cycle payments are made only for genuine underpayment and require HR manager approval.
- Corrections to a finalised pay are adjusted in the following cycle unless an underpayment breaches the award, in which case it is corrected immediately.

For payroll queries, contact the HR/Payroll team only.
