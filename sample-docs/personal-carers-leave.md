# Personal and Carer's Leave Policy

This policy sets out paid personal/carer's leave (commonly called sick leave) and unpaid carer's leave for Wiseway Logistics employees in Australia, in line with the National Employment Standards (NES).

## Your entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to **ten (10) days of paid personal/carer's leave** per year. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their ordinary hours. The leave accrues progressively during the year and any unused balance **carries over** from one year to the next. It is not paid out when you leave Wiseway.

You can use paid personal/carer's leave when you are:

- **unfit for work** because you are sick or injured (personal leave); or
- caring for or supporting a member of your **immediate family or household** who is sick, injured, or affected by an unexpected emergency (carer's leave).

Immediate family includes a spouse or de facto partner, child, parent, grandparent, grandchild, or sibling — and the equivalent relations of your partner.

## Unpaid carer's leave

If you have used all your paid leave, you are entitled to **two (2) days of unpaid carer's leave** for each occasion a family or household member needs care or support.

## Compassionate (bereavement) leave

You are entitled to **two (2) days of paid compassionate leave** each time an immediate family or household member dies or suffers a life-threatening illness or injury. A stillbirth or miscarriage of your child or your current partner's child also qualifies.

## Notifying Wiseway

You must tell your supervisor that you will be absent **as soon as possible** — ideally before the start of your rostered shift. Call the depot directly; a text message alone is not sufficient for shift roles where coverage must be arranged. Tell us the reason and how long you expect to be away so the roster can be adjusted.

## Evidence requirements

Wiseway may ask for **reasonable evidence** that the leave was taken for a permitted reason. Acceptable evidence includes:

- a medical certificate from a registered medical or health practitioner; or
- a statutory declaration where a certificate cannot reasonably be obtained.

As a general rule, evidence is required for absences of **two (2) or more consecutive days**, and for any single-day absence either side of a public holiday or rostered day off. Your manager may request evidence for shorter absences where there is a pattern of concern. Leave may be unpaid if reasonable evidence is requested and not provided.

For questions, contact the HR team or your line manager.
